## The People Behind AdvitSpace

Small team, senior people. When you work with us, you speak directly to the experts doing the job.

{{< figure src="/images/jane-doe.jpg" alt="Portrait of Jane Doe" width="240" >}}

### Jane Doe — Co-Founder & Managing Director

Jane spent a decade in corporate IT security before founding AdvitSpace in 2019, including five years leading the security operations team at a FTSE 250 financial services firm. She holds CISSP and Cyber Essentials assessor certifications. Jane handles client strategy, compliance engagements, and makes sure every report we produce passes the "would my gran understand this?" test.

Outside work she's a keen hill walker and an unapologetic advocate for strong coffee.

{{< figure src="/images/john-smith.jpg" alt="Portrait of John Smith" width="240" >}}

### John Smith — Co-Founder & Technical Director

John is the hands-on technical half of the team: penetration testing, infrastructure hardening, and cloud architecture. He previously ran infrastructure for a healthcare SaaS provider and is OSCP-certified. If it has an IP address, John has probably tried to break into it (ethically, and mostly with permission).

Away from the keyboard, John restores vintage bicycles — a hobby he insists is just "penetration testing with spanners."
