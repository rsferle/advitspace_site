### Remus Sferle — Cyber Assessor & Consultant


{{< figure src="/images/remus.jpg" alt="Portrait of Remus Sferle" width="400" >}}

As Cyber Assessor and Consultant at Advitspace, I combine hands-on cybersecurity knowledge with a practical, client-focused approach to security assessment, compliance and cyber risk.

My role centres on conducting IASME Cyber Essentials and Cyber Assurance assessments, working directly with organisations to understand their systems, security controls and processes, identify gaps and provide practical guidance for remediation.

For Cyber Essentials, I assess the implementation of the core technical controls and support organisations in preparing for certification. For Cyber Assurance, I take a broader view, considering governance, risk management, policies, people, processes and continual improvement.

As a consultant, I help clients translate cybersecurity and compliance requirements into clear, achievable actions that are appropriate to their organisation and risk profile. My work covers areas including secure configuration, access control, vulnerability management, network security, asset management and security governance.

I support clients from initial gap assessments and readiness through remediation, certification and ongoing assurance, with a focus on practical improvements rather than simply meeting a checklist.

Before moving into cybersecurity, I trained and practised as a surgeon within the NHS. This experience shapes my approach to security through an appreciation of operational pressure, complex and legacy environments, attention to detail and the real-world consequences of system failures.

I enjoy working where clinical-grade attention to detail meets practical cybersecurity — assessing security objectively, explaining risk clearly and helping organisations build stronger, sustainable security practices.
