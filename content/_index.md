## Who We Are

AdvitSpace is a UK-based IT and cybersecurity consultancy founded in 2019. What started as a two-person operation helping small businesses manage their networks has grown into a trusted partner for organisations across finance, healthcare, and professional services.

We believe security shouldn't be a luxury. Our mission is to make enterprise-grade protection accessible and understandable for businesses of every size — no jargon, no fear-selling, just practical solutions that fit the way you work.

## What We Do

### Cyber Essentials & Cyber Essentials PLUS
We guide you through the UK's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) certification scheme end to end — gap analysis, technical remediation, and assessment submission. For Cyber Essentials PLUS, we handle the technical verification and vulnerability scans required for full certification.

### Managed IT Services
Proactive monitoring, patching, and helpdesk support. We keep your systems healthy so your team can focus on the business.

### Penetration Testing & Vulnerability Assessments
Regular testing of your external and internal infrastructure, with clear, prioritised remediation advice — not a 200-page PDF nobody reads.

### Cloud & Infrastructure
Migration, configuration, and hardening of Microsoft 365, Azure, and on-premise environments, built with security by design.

### Security Awareness Training
Human error causes most breaches. We deliver engaging training and simulated phishing campaigns that actually change behaviour.
